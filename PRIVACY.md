# Privacy

Privacy Display does not collect, store off-device, or transmit any personal data.

* No internet permission, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.
* The app never captures screenshots, records the display, or reads screen contents.
* Settings (intensity, color, options) are stored locally on your device in app storage.
* If you enable optional brightness dimming, your previous brightness values are stored locally only to restore them.

Permissions: display over other apps (draws the filter), foreground service + notifications (keeps the filter alive and offers a Stop button), modify system settings (only for the optional brightness feature).
